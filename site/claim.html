How NearBase trust works — the honest version

On NearBase, you own your facts: tuition, hours, photos, programs. Nobody can describe your school better than you. But quality scores come only from families and teachers — schools can't grade themselves here, and neither can your competitors. That separation is why families trust what they read, and why a claimed profile is worth more than any ad.

Questions for the tour

NearBase school tour kit. Bring this on paper. Write the answer in the margin. The school’s name goes on the line, not ours.

School: ______________________________   Date: ______________   Who showed you around: ______________

A calm room is not the same thing as a good fit for a duty day. Ask the practical questions even if the classroom feels right.

1. The room

  1. Where do children get materials themselves, and what is kept for a teacher to hand out?
  2. How long can a child stay with one piece of work before the day moves on?
  3. Are ages mixed in the room, or is everyone the same age?
  4. What does a teacher do when a child is concentrating and another child needs help?
  5. Where is the rest space, and is rest required even for a child who does not nap?
  6. What does the room look like at 7:30am and at 5:30pm, not only at the hour of this tour?

2. The day

  1. Walk me through drop-off to pickup for a child my child’s age.
  2. What is outside every day, and what happens when it rains or the air is bad?
  3. How do you handle a child who is new, shy, or having a hard week?
  4. How do children practice pouring, dressing, washing, and cleaning up?
  5. What does a disagreement between children look like here?
  6. May I see the posted daily rhythm, not only hear it described?

3. Hours

  1. What is the earliest drop-off and the latest pickup on a normal day?
  2. Is the morning program a half day, a school day, or a full day?
  3. What do you charge, or how do you handle it, if duty runs past closing?
  4. Which days are closed that a normal work calendar is not (training days, conferences, snow)?
  5. If we only need a school day now, can we add early drop-off later without losing the seat?

4. Cost, fee assistance, and MCCYN

  1. What is the tuition for my child’s age, and what else is billed (meals, supplies, late pickup, annual fees)?
  2. Do you accept MCCYN or DoD fee assistance? Who submits the paperwork, you or us?
  3. Is a military discount the same as MCCYN here, or a separate reduction?
  4. What happens to the fee if orders change, a deployment starts, or we PCS out mid-year?
  5. Do you offer a free seat, subsidy, scholarship, or sliding scale? What makes a family eligible?
  6. Can you show the fee page in writing before we leave?

5. PCS and the waitlist

  1. Do you take children mid-year, or only at a set start?
  2. If we are on a waitlist at another school, does any of that time transfer here?
  3. How do siblings affect the list?
  4. What should we bring from our orders, and when do you need it?
  5. If we leave on PCS, what do you give the next school (records, portfolio, waitlist notes)?
  6. Who do we call if an EFMP or special-needs plan needs to be in place before the first day?

6. Before you say yes

  1. Did you see children working, or only a room set up for visitors?
  2. Did the hours you need exist on a written schedule?
  3. Did someone name the fee, the assistance they accept, and what is not included?
  4. Do you know what happens if you arrive off-cycle?

Illustrative guidance for a tour. It is not a promise about any school in the NearBase directory.

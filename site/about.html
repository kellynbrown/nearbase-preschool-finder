The story

Finding the right preschool shouldn't depend on who you know.

Twelve years teaching in NCR classrooms, built on one belief: every child deserves a great start — not just the ones whose families stumble onto the right school.

Then military spouse life made the other side familiar: the scramble. New orders, new city, no network — and the search starting from zero, again.

"The moment I knew the NearBase Preschool Finder needed to exist was when I saw how many great preschools were invisible to the families who needed them most."

The schools weren't the problem — extraordinary small Montessori programs were everywhere. They were just invisible to anyone searching from a thousand miles away.

NearBase runs on direct, present-day knowledge: the directors, the classrooms, the waitlists that never make it onto a website.

"I want every military parent who uses NearBase to feel seen. Like the platform actually understands their life — the deployments, the moves, the uncertainty, and the love."

That knowledge shouldn't live in one head. Now it doesn't.

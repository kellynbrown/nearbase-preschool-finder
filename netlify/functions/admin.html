Monthly data sweep

Your non-negotiable rule: the top 20 schools get verified once a month. Call or check each one — waitlist status, tuition, hours — then mark it swept. Anything you change goes in via the edit button.

Registry cross-check

Checks your schools against the HIFLD federal child-care facility registry (state-sourced records: capacity, phone, facility type). Strong matches can be accepted in one tap; uncertain ones wait for your judgment. Accepted data publishes into schools.js and appears on each school's profile page.
